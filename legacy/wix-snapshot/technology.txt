OUR MISSION
Empowering Innovation,Shaping Tomorrow.
At Apricity Digital Labs, our mission is to harness the power of AI and digital innovation to create smart, scalable solutions that enhance urban living and business growth. We are committed to developing technologies that simplify complex challenges, making life more efficient and connected. Our focus is on delivering user-friendly apps, data-driven insights, and responsive web designs that empower businesses and communities to thrive in an ever-evolving digital landscape.
​
We aim to drive progress through technology, ensuring that our solutions contribute to sustainable and intelligent growth.
History
Founded by Mr. Sunpreet Singh, whose Indian Army background shaped the company’s ethos of discipline and innovation, Apricity Digital Labs began with the goal of using AI to enhance urban efficiency. Starting with local business solutions, it soon expanded into larger projects like smart traffic systems and AI-driven waste management.
​
The launch of the Insstanto app was a key milestone, bringing on-demand cleaning services to urban residents. Today, Apricity is a leader in developing AI technologies that promote smarter, sustainable urban living, continually evolving to meet global challenges.
